Maknon Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Applies to the Maknon app for iPhone (bundle com.eqrra.maknoon), including TestFlight builds.

Maknon is a free Quran recitation app published by EQRAA FIRST INVESTMENT COMPANY, Riyadh 34422, Saudi Arabia ("we"). There is no sign-up or login, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and Maknon does not track you. Most of what it remembers stays on your phone. This page explains the few things that leave it.

In short

  • While you recite, your voice is streamed live to our recognition server so Maknon can follow you and point out mistakes. The server stores nothing: no audio, no transcripts, no results and no logs.
  • Quran text, translations, tafsir, recitations and fonts are downloaded from QuranHub. Those requests carry no name or account.
  • If you join a group, the groups server receives a display name and, only if you turn sharing on, your daily totals.
  • Everything else (sessions, mistakes, progress, goals, bookmarks, settings and your saved recordings) stays on your phone. A backup file you export yourself holds most of it, but not your recordings (section 6).

1. Your recitation audio

Maknon uses the microphone only when you start a recitation session, a test or a voice search, and only after you allow microphone access. While it listens, the app streams your voice live over an encrypted connection (WSS) to the Nabr recognition server run by MISRAJ AI at nabr.misraj.ai. Along with the audio it sends the place you start from (surah, ayah and word) and your chosen strictness levels for recitation and tajweed. It does not send your name, a user ID or a device ID.

The server uses the audio only to recognise your recitation and send the results back to you in real time. It stores nothing: no audio, no transcripts, no results and no logs. When the session, test or search ends, nothing about it is left on the server. Because nothing is kept, the app does not need to ask for consent to store recordings, and it has no such option.

Voice search works the same way: the audio is streamed to the same server to find the ayah you recited, and nothing is recorded or saved for it, on the server or on your phone.

We plan to move recognition to qurani.ai's Nabr server (api.qurani.ai). We will update this policy before a version that uses it is released.

Recordings saved on your phone

By default Maknon also saves the audio of each session and test on your phone, so you can replay sessions, hear mistake clips and share a session. These recordings are never uploaded by the app. They are deleted after 30 days, and the oldest go first once they take more than 500 MB. You can change both limits, turn saving off (Settings › Privacy and data › Data usage › Keep session audio) or delete every recording at once (Settings › Delete all audio).

2. Quran content (QuranHub)

Maknon downloads the Quran text, Mushaf pages and fonts, translations, tafsir, word data and recitations from QuranHub by qurani.ai (api.quranhub.com) and its content delivery network (quranhub.b-cdn.net). When you search the Quran, the words you type are sent to QuranHub to get results. These requests carry no name, account, recording or device ID. Like any web request, they show your IP address and which content you asked for, and qurani.ai's own terms apply to them.

3. Groups and leaderboards

Groups let you read with family and friends, share a khatma and see group and public leaderboards. They are optional. They run on a database hosted for MISRAJ AI on Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). When you first create or join a group, the app signs in anonymously: the server creates a random user ID with no e-mail, phone or password, and the app keeps the sign-in token on your phone. The groups server then stores:

  • the display name you type (up to 40 characters; it can be any name you like), which members of your groups can see;
  • groups you create (name, emoji, description, public or private, invite code), your memberships and role, notices you post, juz you claim in a shared khatma, and reports you make (reason and an optional note);
  • only if you turn on Share daily totals: one total per day of minutes, pages, ayahs and completed khatmas. Other members see your totals per week, Hijri month or all time, not a day-by-day list. Public leaderboards show totals per group, never per person;
  • as on any server: your IP address and device type (user agent) with each sign-in session, in an access log and in the hosting provider's request logs.

The groups server never receives your recordings, your mistakes or anything you recite.

4. What stays on your phone

Your sessions, mistakes, word and page progress, memorisation and review schedule, tests, goals, streaks and achievements, bookmarks, settings, display name, saved recordings and downloaded content are stored on your phone, in the app's database and files. A backup file you export yourself carries most of it, but not the recordings or the downloaded content (section 6). If your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer backup) is on, it can include this data, as it does for other apps.

Reminders, when you turn them on, are scheduled on your phone. Maknon does not use push notifications and does not register a push token.

5. What we do not do

  • No sign-up, ads, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell or rent data, and we do not combine it with data from other companies.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

6. When you share something

Maknon sends a recording, a session card or badge image, ayah text, a group invite, a backup file or a diagnostics file only when you share it yourself through the iPhone share sheet or copy it. Where it goes is up to you. A backup file holds your history (sessions, mistakes, word progress, bookmarks, khatma progress, memorisation and review cards, goals, test results, when you shared verses, and the length and date of each saved recording) and most of your settings, including your display name, recent searches and recently read pages. It holds no audio. The file is not encrypted: it leaves your phone only where you save or send it, and anyone who has it can read it. Links you open (for example to qurani.ai) open in Safari's in-app view under that site's own terms.

8. Children

Maknon is for readers of all ages and does not ask for age. It has no ads or tracking. The only place where someone can type text that others see is groups (display names, group names and notices). Younger children should use groups with a parent. If you believe a child has shared personal information there, write to us and we will remove it.

9. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • On your phone: recordings follow the limits in section 1. To delete everything, use You › Erase all data on this device, or delete the app.
  • Recitation audio: nothing is stored on the server (section 1).
  • Groups: turning off Share daily totals deletes every total you shared. You can leave a group, and an owner can delete a group with its notices and board. Erase all data on this device also deletes your groups data from the server (your name, memberships, notices, totals, claims and reports; groups you own pass to another member, or are deleted if no one is left). Anonymous users who do not open groups for 180 days are deleted with their data, and the sign-in access log is cleared after 30 days. The hosting provider's request logs follow its own retention.
  • If you deleted the app before erasing, we cannot link your anonymous groups data to you. It is deleted automatically after 180 days. Write to us if you need it removed sooner and we will help as far as we can identify it.

10. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS and WSS). Group data is protected by row-level security on the server, so members read only what their groups allow.

11. Your rights and contact

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Because Maknon has no sign-up, most of your data is only on your phone and under your control.

مكنون لكل الأعمار، ولا يسأل عن العمر، وليس فيه إعلانات ولا تتبّع. والمكان الوحيد الذي يكتب فيه أحد نصًا يراه غيره هو المجموعات (الأسماء الظاهرة وأسماء المجموعات والإعلانات). ويحسُن أن يستخدم الأطفال الصغار المجموعات مع أحد والديهم. وإن رأيت أن طفلًا شارك فيها معلومات شخصية فراسلنا ونحذفها.

٩. مدة الاحتفاظ وطريقة الحذف

  • على جوالك: تتبع التسجيلات الحدود المذكورة في القسم ١. ولحذف كل شيء استخدم: ملفّي ‹ امسح كل البيانات من هذا الجهاز، أو احذف التطبيق.
  • صوت التلاوة: لا يُحفَظ منه شيء على الخادم (القسم ١).
  • المجموعات: إيقاف شارك مجاميعك اليومية يحذف كل ما شاركته من مجاميع. ويمكنك مغادرة المجموعة، ولمالك المجموعة حذفها بإعلاناتها ولوحتها. و«امسح كل البيانات من هذا الجهاز» يحذف كذلك بيانات مجموعاتك من الخادم (الاسم والعضويات والإعلانات والمجاميع والأجزاء المحجوزة والبلاغات؛ وتنتقل المجموعات التي تملكها إلى عضو آخر، أو تُحذَف إن لم يبقَ فيها أحد). ويُحذَف المستخدم المجهول الذي لا يفتح المجموعات ١٨٠ يومًا مع بياناته، ويُمسَح سجل الدخول بعد ٣٠ يومًا. أما سجلات الطلبات لدى مزوّد الاستضافة فتتبع مدة احتفاظه.
  • إن حذفت التطبيق قبل المسح فلا نستطيع ربط بيانات مجموعاتك المجهولة بك، وتُحذَف تلقائيًا بعد ١٨٠ يومًا. وإن احتجت حذفها قبل ذلك فراسلنا ونساعدك بقدر ما نستطيع التعرّف عليها.
